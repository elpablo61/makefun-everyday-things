## The entry

Line 1 explains a toaster to a 10-year-old in one vivid sentence built on a single
analogy. Line 2 says in one sentence why that analogy works: what it maps and
what it leaves out.

> In Ibu Sari's kitchen at seven, a toaster is a tiny elevator that carries one slice of bread down into a hot room and pops it back up brown and smelling of toast.
> It maps the ride down, the heat, and the pop back up, but leaves out that the bread never presses a button and the little room has no windows.

305 characters, two lines, one analogy.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

when that variable is set). The program refuses to write an entry outside the
gate's 1–400 character range.
